# obsidian-second-brain

## For future agent
This note describes obsidian-second-brain, a tool Company Brain depends on (as of 2026-10). obsidian-second-brain (https://github.com/eugeniughelbur/obsidian-second-brain, MIT) is the single-person AI second-brain toolkit Company Brain is built on. Company Brain vendors its AI-first rules, write validator, and vault MCP server, and adds team and company layers. It can keep running on a personal vault alongside Company Brain.
