# Now-Next-Later Roadmap

## For future agent
This note explains the Now-Next-Later roadmap (Janna Bastow, ProdPad): an outcome-based roadmap with three time horizons instead of dates, and how Company Brain keeps it in sync with discovery, prioritization, OKRs, and projects.

## What it is
A roadmap format popularized by Janna Bastow at ProdPad (https://www.prodpad.com/blog/invented-now-next-later-roadmap/, as of 2026-10) that replaces a date-based timeline with three horizons:
- **Now**: what the team is working on, with high certainty and detail.
- **Next**: what comes after, shaped and broadly understood, still open to change.
- **Later**: problems and opportunities worth solving, deliberately loose.

Each item states the problem or outcome, not just the feature, so the roadmap communicates intent and leaves room for discovery to change the solution.

## How to use it
1. Fill Now from committed, scoped work (quick wins, planned slices of big bets).
2. Fill Next from validated solutions and the top of the prioritization matrix.
3. Fill Later with important opportunities not yet explored.
4. Review at least monthly and at quarterly planning: move items, drop stale Later items, and keep each card tied to a key result.

## In Company Brain
- Boards in Kanban format: [[Company Roadmap]] and `<Team> Roadmap`, columns Now, Next, Later, Shipped.
- Each card links a solution (`SOL - ...`) or project and names its KR; the note's `roadmap:` field mirrors the column.
- Feeds: the [[Opportunity Solution Tree]] (validated solutions) and the [[Effort-Impact Matrix]] (quick wins and sliced big bets).
- Skill: `/company-brain:roadmap`.
